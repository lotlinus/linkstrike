        How to play
        Link Strike v1.1
        How to playFree-for-all jetpack shooter

        Phone (landscape)

        Left thumb flies: push up to fire the jetpack. Right thumb aims and shoots automatically. The round buttons swap weapon and reload.

        Laptop

        WASD or arrow keys to fly (W, Up or Space = jetpack). Mouse to aim, hold click to shoot. R reloads, Q swaps between your weapon and the Glock.

        The match

        Every player for themselves: first to 10 kills wins, or most kills when the timer runs out. Pick your weapon in the lobby; it is locked during the match. Health packs lie around each map.

        Rooms

        Make a room, send the link, and friends join with it. ALCATRAZ opens every night at 8pm (Nigeria time).
